You pay the models. Hydra takes nothing.

Route everything through a frontier model and you pay frontier prices on every task. Hydra sends each task to the cheapest head that clears the bar — most land on free local models — so the same work costs a fraction. Hydra's own cut of that is, and always will be, zero.

free foreverwhat "free" actually means here

No asterisks.

✓No subscription, no seats. Install it, use it on every machine you own.
✓No usage limits. Hydra never meters your dispatches — the providers do their own billing.
✓No telemetry. Routing, cost logs, and calibration stay on your machine.
✓No lock-in. Provider-neutral by design — swap heads, go fully local, or leave anytime.
✓MIT license. Fork it, ship it, build a business on it. That's allowed.
✓Local floor. With Ollama, the terminal fallback is a free local model — a $0 path always exists.
